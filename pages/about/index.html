HOW I WORK

I like to understand how things work.

My background sits between mathematics, computer science, and data. I enjoy taking a problem apart, understanding what is happening underneath, and then building something practical from it.

I learn by doing. I write code, work with data, test ideas, break things, fix them, and keep digging until the result makes sense. That process has gradually pulled me toward data analysis, statistical modelling, and quantitative research, alongside software development.

I care less about using technology for its own sake and more about what it can help me understand or solve. Whether I’m building an application, cleaning a dataset, studying a time series, or visualizing a result, I want the logic to be clear and the output to be useful.

I’m still learning, and that is part of the work. My goal is to keep building a stronger connection between mathematics, code, and real-world problems.
